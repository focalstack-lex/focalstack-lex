I am a Computer Engineering student (BSCpE) at Cor Jesu College of Digos in the Davao Region, Philippines.

Here on GitHub, I explore the practical side of technology: building native desktop tools, orchestrating local AI agents, and integrating software with low-level systems and creative media.

### What Drives My Work

I like understanding how things work from hardware execution up to user interfaces. That curiosity turns into building clean tools, clear architecture, and native systems that make work feel seamless.

My operating philosophy is **systems over motivation** (influenced by *Atomic Habits*). Rather than relying on temporary inspiration, I design sustainable daily routines, reliable workflows, and modular software architecture.
